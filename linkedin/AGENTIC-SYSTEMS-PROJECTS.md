## Project 2: Marketing Revenue Assurance

Project URL: https://marketing-revenue-assurance.vercel.app

Repository: Private implementation repository

Dates: August 2026 to August 2026

Description:

Built a production-shaped marketing revenue assurance system for agencies that need to find where paid demand stops becoming collected cash. Ten bounded LangGraph specialists validate source contracts, reconcile lead delivery, compare attribution coverage, inspect funnel conversion, detect booked-to-collected revenue gaps, rank incidents, draft recovery proposals, and stop at a human decision gate. Deterministic Python controls own the arithmetic, evidence rules, risk score, budget limits, access policy, and mutation boundary.

The public deployment runs 12 synthetic scenarios across Meta Ads, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, GoHighLevel CRM, call tracking, and settlement exports. Changing the scenario or attribution window changes the evidence, findings, risk, and proposed action. The cash collection scenario identifies a $44,700 booked-to-collected gap, proposes a draft recovery plan, and requires approval without writing to an ad platform, CRM, task system, message channel, or payment system.

Release evidence: 31 automated tests, 12 of 12 golden scenarios, 81.93 percent measured Python coverage, 25 Postman assertions, 13 evaluation metrics at 1.0, 16 Prometheus signal families, two independent Docker and PostgreSQL CI paths, durable audit and checkpoint restart proof, cross-instance signed-receipt approval checks, and verified desktop, mobile, API documentation, healthy, blocked, approval, and rejection journeys.

Technology reasoning: FastAPI provides typed Pydantic contracts and generated OpenAPI. LangGraph provides explicit state, bounded fan-out, checkpoints, interruption, and resumption. PostgreSQL and Alembic provide the durable deployment path. Signed receipts preserve approval integrity on the stateless public Vercel path. OpenTelemetry, Prometheus, structured logs, trace IDs, evaluation fixtures, and Postman expose operational and behavioral evidence. Replay mode keeps the public demo available at zero model cost.

Top 5 skills: LangGraph, Python, FastAPI, Agent Evaluation, Observability
