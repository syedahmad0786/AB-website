Campaign research and copy: Carry client research all the way into campaign copy.

Campaign research and copy connects intake and call context to market research, offer planning, script development and downstream drafting. The finalized script becomes the shared handoff, so website and email copy inherit the campaign’s direction instead of starting over.

Why it matters

Keeps campaign direction coherent across research, scripts and downstream assets while giving reviewers a shared reference point.

Let downstream copy inherit the finalized script

The finalized-VSL source workflow feeds the website and email-copy stage. The documented process uses a finalized-script handoff; the local import is not proof that every native approval gate or destination was independently tested.

Show the whole handoff, not a collage of tools
