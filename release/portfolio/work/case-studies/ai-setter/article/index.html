3 short messages should stay one conversation.

A company-scoped setter workflow joins message context, qualification, knowledge and follow-up state before deciding the next response.

3 messages → 1 context; the conversation-buffering use case, not three separate replies.

Name the system: lead operations and appointment setting

This is the setter workflow, not an unrelated support chatbot. The original archived appointment-setter export has forty-seven nodes, including forty-four non-note nodes, and is shown as a disconnected local import.

Keep the lead and company context together

Incoming message context belongs with the CRM record, the company knowledge and the qualification state. A short burst should not be treated as unrelated conversations just because messages arrive separately.

Decide the next action from the state

The workflow separates qualification, response handling and follow-up state.

Check the destination before calling it complete

The business outcome is a valid next handoff or appointment state, not merely a successful model response. This preview does not claim measured booking lift, client revenue or validated live delivery.
