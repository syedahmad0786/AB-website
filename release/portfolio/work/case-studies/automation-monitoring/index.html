ErrorLens: Make broken automations visible and actionable.

ErrorLens gives teams a clear place to investigate workflow failures. Execution details, severity, suggested next steps and incident ownership sit together, while logging and Slack alert paths bring the failure context into the team’s daily operating rhythm.

1 / 6

What it does

  • Incident review interface with filters, failure detail and assigned ownership.
  • Failure-context views covering classification, severity and suggested next steps.
  • Recovery review controls with incident status and a local resolved state.

Why it matters

Makes failure investigation and responsibility easier to coordinate, with the context needed to decide the next recovery action.

The failure had a business cost

Ahmad reports that the commissioning company lost approximately three clients in two months when automations broke. That is the reason for the system, not proof that the same amount was subsequently saved. The recovered source shows how failures are collected, classified and put in front of the team.

Collect the failure before asking someone to fix it

The original creator workflow uses a schedule or webhook, fetches failed n8n executions, filters recent failures and excludes its own runs. The local capture shows that actual source topology, imported without credentials and never executed.

Classify, log and alert

The source classifies failures into seven categories with severity and a suggested next step. Log to Sheet is an HTTP Request to the Google Sheets API, followed by the Slack alert path. A native Google Sheets node is not required for that logging step.

Make recovery a human-owned decision

The broader ErrorLens interface adds the review surface: filters, incident detail, an owner and a local resolved state. An alert or a local resolved flag does not prove the affected business record was repaired. The responsible person still checks the destination.

Inside the system

  • Cross-platform visibility Documented failure review across n8n, Make and Zapier.
  • Incident owner Connects available failure context to a responsible person.
  • Recovery decision Separates investigation from permission to change a workflow.
  • Destination check Verifies the affected business result after an authorized action.
