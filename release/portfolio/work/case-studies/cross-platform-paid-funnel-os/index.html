Do not confuse a shared trigger with the whole platform

A trigger dispatcher is one layer. The case also identifies the collection, attribution, compilation and next-review responsibilities rather than presenting a seventeen-node dispatcher as the entire system.

Keep the next decision visible

The reporting layer is meant to supply a reviewable next action for the operator and creative team. The source captures establish structure, not current provider authorization, campaign health or causal revenue improvement.
