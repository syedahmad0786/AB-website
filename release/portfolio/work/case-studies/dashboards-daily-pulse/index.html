Member Pulse: See the member journey, not just the latest interaction.

Member Pulse brings a member’s timeline, lifecycle, experience map, interaction stream and tenure into a connected application. Teams can inspect events, explore the inputs behind health signals and view membership patterns across time before choosing their next action.

1 / 5

What it does

  • Timeline and Stages views with platform lanes, date ranges and event detail.
  • Experience Map and Pulse Stream views linking touchpoints, interaction context and inspectable health signals.
  • Tenure views for cohorts, joins, calendars and membership duration.

Why it matters

Helps membership teams interpret interactions in context and prepare more informed follow-up decisions.

How the system is built

  1. SourcesRepresented lanes include Mighty Networks, Intercom, Calendly/Zoom, Stripe and ordering tools.
  2. IngestionDesigned provider webhooks and scheduled reconciliation; removed from the private restoration.
  3. NormalizationMember identity, source IDs, event types and timestamps.
  4. StorageRecommended Postgres member-event ledger and versioned signal snapshots.
  5. DecisionsSource-defined health inputs, qualification gates and human interpretation.
  6. DashboardOriginal standalone HTML, JavaScript, CSS and SVG application.
  7. Write-backsNone in the restoration; connected follow-up would require separate approval.

The dashboard

12 views: Today, Member summary, Member list, Member timeline, Pulse stream, Health scorecard, At-risk, Ambassadors, Ordering activity, Equipment purchases, Cohort retention, Events and calls.

The member river: ninety days of one member across community, ordering, support, calls and billing, with the daily churn score drawn above the lanes and gate events marked where they happened.

State lives in the URL (view, filters, date range), records open in drawers with their history, and every action that changes something is written to an activity log and is safe to repeat.

Data model

  • members(id, external_key, joined_at, lifecycle_stage)
  • identities(id, member_id, provider, external_id)
  • interactions(id, member_id, source, event_type, occurred_at)
  • signal_definitions(id, version, name, churn_weight, lead_weight)
  • signal_values(id, member_id, definition_id, value, observed_at)
  • score_snapshots(id, member_id, rule_version, churn_score, lead_score)
  • membership_periods(id, member_id, starts_at, ends_at, tier)
  • review_actions(id, member_id, reviewer_id, decision, evidence_refs).

Rules in the backend

  1. Preserve the source-defined 12-signal model rather than substituting a generic engagement score.
  2. The displayed weighted formula is Σ(signal × weight) ÷ Σweights × 10, subject to validated input ranges.
  3. Apply qualification hard gates separately from weighted scores; a favorable average cannot override a failed gate.
  4. Missing evidence remains unknown, not automatically zero or healthy.
  5. Compute tenure from membership intervals; overlapping intervals must not double-count duration.

Storage

Recommend Supabase Postgres with tenant/member RLS for a future connected backend. Proposed retention is 90 days for raw interactions and 24 months for reviewed aggregates/audits. Minimize support-message content, mask personal emails and restrict agreement/refund information.

Reliability

Deduplicate source events, reconcile identities and recompute snapshots for late arrivals. A connected UI should show per-source freshness, unavailable signals and partial history. The current restoration must remain labeled local, not “Synced” to providers.

Security and access

Designed roles separate member-support operators, administrators and read-only stakeholders. No provider secrets belong in standalone HTML. Public release and human privacy clearance remain separate requirements.

Cost

Provider subscriptions, API access, event volume and future storage/processing incur possible costs. No retention improvement or commercial uplift is established.

Read the story before deciding the next step

The timeline keeps platform lanes and dated interactions together. Open an event to inspect its detail, or change the date range to narrow the story.

Move between lifecycle and lived experience

Stages follows the journey from Lead to Advocate. Experience Map places the persona, touchpoints, wants, frustrations and source matrix together.

A score needs its inputs

Pulse Stream combines the interaction thread with the source-defined 12-signal health model. Churn risk, lead qualification, hard gates and signal notes remain inspectable.

Look across time, not just at one member

Reset returns the walkthrough to its starting state. No API calls, provider connections or persistent member updates are made.
