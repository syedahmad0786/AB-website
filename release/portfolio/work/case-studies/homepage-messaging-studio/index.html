Messaging

Homepage messaging studio: Turn client research into a clear homepage direction.

Homepage messaging studio connects market research, a dedicated messaging map and staged copy workflows. Client material becomes a shared direction document before drafting, giving the team a concrete reference for developing homepage copy and carrying that direction into final-copy preparation.

1 / 2

What it does

  • Staged workflow family covering research, messaging maps, copy and final-copy preparation.
  • Messaging-map workflow connecting stored client research with a separate direction document.
  • Linked Airtable and Google Docs handoffs for carrying client context into copy development.

Why it matters

Gives writers a shared messaging foundation and a structured path from research to homepage copy.

Inside the system

  • Call and page-plan intake Parses Fathom context and the structured-plaintext Octopus plan into linked client records.
  • Research document Turns stored client material into an inspectable market-research artifact.
  • Messaging map Creates a separate messaging-direction document after research.
  • Three homepage variants Produces linked copy alternatives and corresponding Google Docs.
  • Approval inbox Lets the team or client choose a direction and records approval status.
