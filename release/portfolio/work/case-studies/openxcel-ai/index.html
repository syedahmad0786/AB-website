OpenXCEL AI: Give your business an AI coworker with company context.

OpenXCEL AI brings company memory, editable skills, tools and approval boundaries into a business-owned AI workspace. Built around a self-hosted Slack coworker, it gives teams a practical way to organize context and define how delegated work should be reviewed.

1 / 2

What it does

  • Company-context workspace with retained memory and editable skills.
  • Self-hosted Slack coworker setup for business task intake and tool use.
  • Connection setup and approval boundaries tied to the intended task and reviewer.

Why it matters

Provides a company-specific foundation for delegating bounded work while keeping context and review responsibilities together.
