Inside the system

  • Source knowledge base Collects configured competitor and publication material, with deeper analysis of selected videos.
  • Idea curation board Lets editors save, refine and select source-based or researched candidates.
  • Scheduled topic research Targets three to five daily candidates as a specification, not measured output.
  • Format-specific generation Routes an account, selected idea, format and requirements to the appropriate writing workflow.
  • Editorial handoff Links drafts to the profile for human review; automatic publishing is not documented.
