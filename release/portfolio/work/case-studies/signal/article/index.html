Track the path from answer to visit to conversion

A mention is not a sale. The useful operating path is to agree buyer questions, capture dated answers, inspect the cited sources, then examine identifiable referral and conversion evidence before prioritizing an action.

Why the commercial question matters

In its June 16, 2025 own-site study, Ahrefs reported 0.5% of visits and 12.1% of signups from AI search over a thirty-day window, with a 23-times conversion-per-visit comparison against traditional organic search. These are Ahrefs’ figures, not SIGNAL’s results and not revenue.

Do not turn an industry into a promise

Ahrefs’ separate analysis of 81,947 sites in May–June 2025 found more mixed user behavior, including shallower browsing and more bounces from AI referrals. The relevant claim is that referral quality is worth measuring, not that every business will receive the same uplift.
