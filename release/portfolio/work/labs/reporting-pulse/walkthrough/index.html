    Workstream 2Scheduled outputs

    Daily pulse in Slack

    Pulse is the module inside this reporting service: a morning Slack summary, an alert when a metric leaves its agreed range, and weekly report drafts that wait for a person to approve them.

    1. 08:00

      Daily pulse

      Yesterday's spend, leads, bookings and revenue land in the channel your team already reads.

      Internal
    2. Any time

      Anomaly alert

      When a metric breaks its agreed range or a source stops refreshing, the owner is pinged with what changed.

      Internal
    3. Monday

      Weekly report draft

      A weekly report is drafted per team or per client from the same agreed numbers.

      Draft only
    4. Before sending

      A person approves

      Nothing goes to a client until a person has read and approved it.

      Person approves
    5. Month end

      Month-end summary

      A month-end summary of spend, leads, bookings and revenue, with notes on any data gaps.

      Internal

    Client-facing reports always wait for a person to approve themEvery step runs on a schedule

    One pulse, one place

    Each morning the team gets a short summary: what was spent, what came in, what booked and what earned. If a source is late, the pulse says so instead of quietly leaving it out.
